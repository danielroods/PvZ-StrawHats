Plants vs. Zombies 2

Plants vs. Zombies 2 is a tower-defense game built around the simple idea of defending your home with plants against increasingly unusual waves of zombies. Players collect sunlight, place plants strategically, and combine different defensive abilities to create a formation capable of surviving each wave.

Every plant has a specific role on the battlefield. Some provide resources, some attack from a distance, while others slow, block, burn, freeze, or otherwise disrupt approaching enemies. Successful gameplay depends on understanding these roles and placing each plant in the correct position at the correct time.

The game expands this basic formula through different worlds, environments, enemies, and gameplay mechanics. Each environment can introduce new challenges that require players to adapt their strategy rather than relying on a single fixed team of plants.

A major part of the experience is experimentation. Different combinations of plants can produce dramatically different results, and a strategy that works in one level may be ineffective in another. Managing limited resources and understanding enemy behavior are both essential parts of the experience.

This project recreates the Plants vs. Zombies style of gameplay while extending it with additional environments, multiplayer functionality, account progression, and custom systems implemented specifically for the project.
